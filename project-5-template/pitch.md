## Ideation

### Ideas

1. **Free virtual tabletop (VTT)** combining Roll20 and D&D Beyond features: shared map, tokens, fog of war, dice, character sheets. Appeals as a free alternative to paywalled tools. Feasibility: real-time multiplayer sync is a large risk for a solo five-week build, and the feature list is very broad.
2. **Free D&D character sheet builder** with a large catalog of species and subclasses, auto-calculated stats, click-to-roll dice, multiple saved characters and PDF export. Appeals as the part of D&D Beyond most people hit a paywall on. Feasibility: needs no server; the main concerns are content licensing and handling two rules editions.

### Choice

Idea 2. It is more focused, can be finished and polished in five weeks, and needs no backend. Set aside: the VTT (map, tokens, real-time sync), which could be a future direction.

### Branding

Four directions were considered: Open Tome (parchment and ink blue, book with a d20), Nat20 (charcoal, teal and orange, flat d20), Free Hero / Hearthsheet (earthy reds, shield with a flame) and Sheetforge (steel gray, ember orange and cool blue, anvil with a d20 spark). The Developer chose Sheetforge.

## Pitch

Keep this section to about 1,000 words.

### Concept

A free website for building Dungeons & Dragons 5e characters without a paywall. Users pick a rules edition (2014 or 2024) when they start a character, then choose species, class, subclass and background from a catalog. The sheet calculates its own numbers, lets users click a skill, save or attack to roll the dice, keeps multiple characters, and exports a PDF. Users can also create their own homebrew species and subclasses.

### Title and branding

**Sheetforge.** The logo is a glowing ember-orange d20 in flames above a steel-gray anvil with cool blue edge highlights (draft files in `branding/`: dark-background logo, light-background logo, and an icon). The look is dark steel gray backgrounds with ember orange for rolls and key actions and cool blue as a secondary highlight, with a sturdy sans for headings and a plain sans for forms. The tone is crafting: you forge your character. The light version is for PDFs and the icon is for small sizes; the icon still needs a check at favicon size. The draft logo was generated with ChatGPT; OpenAI's current usage terms still need to be checked and recorded in the credits. These may change during development; the current branding will be kept with the visual rules in `spec.md`.

### Why this project

I play a lot of D&D, and my biggest frustration with character-building sites is that most of the interesting species and subclasses are behind a paywall. You have to buy content before you can use it. I want my character sheet to be easy and accessible, and I can't get the species or subclass I want. My friends and I all share this frustration, and I expect it is common across the D&D community.

I chose this over a virtual tabletop because there are already many good VTTs. Most of my personal problems come from building the sheet, so that is where I can add the most. The goal for the five weeks is a site with a large range of options that nobody has to pay for, and that makes building a character sheet easier. Because Wizards of the Coast owns most paywalled content, the catalog will be built from openly licensed sources plus user-made homebrew, and the site will say clearly where each option comes from.

### Users

D&D players and Dungeon Masters, new and experienced, who want to make and keep characters for free, on a laptop or phone. They need correct automatic math, quick dice rolls at the table, several characters kept in one place, and a printable sheet.

### Experience goals

- **Notice:** a large catalog of species and subclasses with nothing locked, and a clear label showing where each option comes from.
- **Feel:** confident that the numbers are right, and in control of the build instead of fighting the tool. The forge theme should make building a character feel like crafting.
- **Be able to:** build a character quickly, roll with one click at the table (on a phone as well as a laptop), keep several characters, and print or save the sheet as a PDF, all without paying or making an account.

### Technical goals

- A data model that handles both the 2014 and 2024 rules. The two differ in structure, for example 2024 ability score bonuses come from the background, not the species. This is the main technical challenge.
- An auto-calculation engine verified against hand-calculated test characters.
- A catalog built from openly licensed sources, each entry tagged with its source and license.
- Click-to-roll dice with advantage and disadvantage, and a roll history.
- Local multi-character storage with JSON export and import.
- A homebrew creator for species and subclasses, with content packs users can import and export as JSON. SRD and homebrew entries share one data model and are labeled by source.
- PDF export from a layout designed for this project.

### Starting point

From scratch for the app. Content comes from openly licensed sources. SRD 5.1 and SRD 5.2 are CC-BY-4.0 according to secondary sources, and the primary documents and each additional source must be checked before use. Open5e's API (SRD 2014, SRD 2024 and third-party documents, each tagged with its license) is a candidate data source, and its license and terms must be checked before use.

Working with AI: I will direct the AI agent with a written spec, check its work by running the site, and verify the character math myself against hand-calculated test characters. I will check every content source and license directly, and I will not accept an AI claim about a rule or license as evidence on its own.

### Tech stack

A static front-end web app with no server, hosted free and publicly (for example GitHub Pages).
- **TypeScript:** catches mistakes such as wrong stat names before the code runs, which matters for character math.
- **React:** builds the sheet from reusable pieces and updates the screen automatically when a value changes, so changing a score recalculates the whole sheet.
- **Vite:** bundles the code and gives a fast local preview.
- **Browser storage:** characters are saved on the user's device, with no accounts or database, so no user data leaves the device. JSON export is the backup.
- **PDF library:** not yet chosen; two or three will be tested in Phase 4.

Vite is new to me. I will learn it as I set up the project, and I expect it to be low risk because it mainly provides a preview server and bundling.

### MVP or critical function

A user can choose the 2024 rules, build a level 1 character from SRD species, class and background, see the auto-calculated sheet, click to roll a skill check, and reload the page with the character still saved. The riskiest part to prove first is the data model working for both editions. Success means test characters match hand calculations in each edition.

### Five-week map

- **Phase 1 (to Nov 2):** pitch and approval; check SRD and source licenses.
- **Phase 2 (Nov 2–9):** the MVP above for one edition, including the data model (built to hold homebrew entries).
- **Phase 3 (Nov 9–16):** second edition, more classes and levels, multiple characters, JSON export, homebrew creator and content-pack import and export.
- **Phase 4 (Nov 16–30):** additional openly licensed catalog content, PDF export, click-to-roll polish, accessibility and mobile; version ready for peer review.
- **Phase 5 (Nov 30–Dec 7):** peer-review fixes, deploy, final verification, breakdown and debrief.

### Stretch goals

In priority order: dice animation; level-up helper; damage rolls and conditions.

### Risks

- **Licensing:** whether subclasses and enough species are available under open licenses. Check the SRD PDFs and each source's terms during Phase 1.
- **Two rules editions:** the data model may need rework. Prove it early in Phase 2.
- **Catalog size:** each SRD has only about 9 species and 12 subclasses (one per class). The homebrew creator and content packs are core scope for this reason, so catalog size depends partly on what users add.
- **Scope:** the creator adds work to Phase 3. Decide early what is cut if it slips.
- **PDF quality:** layout differences between screen and PDF. Test early in Phase 4.
